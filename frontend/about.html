🩸 About Blood Donation Management System

Connecting blood donors with patients who need blood

About the System

The Blood Donation Management System is a web-based application designed to simplify and improve the management of blood donors and blood requests.

The system helps administrators manage donor registrations, approve donors, manage blood requests, check blood compatibility, and find suitable matches between donors and patients.

Its main purpose is to make the process of finding compatible blood donors faster, easier, and more organized.

Key Features

🩸

Donor Management

Register and manage blood donors and their information.

🏥

Blood Requests

Hospitals can submit blood requests for patients who require blood.

🔎

Matching Requests

Find approved blood requests that are compatible with a donor's blood group.

⚖️

Compatibility Checking

Check which blood groups can donate to or receive from another blood group.

👨‍💼

Admin Management

Administrators can approve, edit, reject, and manage donors and requests.

📄

Matching Reports

Generate professional PDF reports containing matching blood requests.

Our Purpose

The goal of this system is to support efficient blood donation management by connecting suitable donors and blood requests based on blood-group compatibility and location.

By organizing donor and patient information in one system, it can help administrators quickly identify potential matches and manage the blood donation process more effectively.
